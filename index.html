Unbroken Temperature Control from Port Discharge to Warehouse.

Produce damage in India occurs primarily during the port-to-cold store transition. MHT Fresh eliminates thermal exposure through dedicated customs clearing agents and pre-booked cold-storage docking.

01

40RF CA Inward Protocols

Continuous temperature data loggers (Sensitech / TempTale) deployed in all inward containers. Real-time verification of pulp temperature prior to customs de-stuffing.

02

Expedited JNPT CFS Clearance

Pre-arrival filing of Bills of Entry. Plant Quarantine (PQ) sampling and FSSAI Authorized Officer inspections expedited within 24 hours of vessel docking.

03

Multi-Chamber Cold Facilities

Temperature-stratified storage nodes in Taloja, Navi Mumbai (Vashi APMC belt), and Pune. High-humidity chambers tailored specifically to pitahaya, grapes, and citrus.
